                About Re:Solution

                Re:Solution is resolution plus solving a problem: it makes small pictures big and sharp, and videos 4K. It's free and open source (MIT licence).

                Your images stay on this computer unless you choose a cloud mode.

                  Use it from the command line

                  For power users: the same engine runs in Terminal on a Mac, from the app's folder (usually Applications › Re-Solution in your home folder):

                  .venv/bin/python -m upscaler image photo.jpg --size 8k --mode faithful
.venv/bin/python -m upscaler video clip.mov --mode fast

                  On Windows, open PowerShell in the app's folder and use .venv\Scripts\python instead. Add --help to see every choice.
